SCENE 1 - THE LAMP ROOM. NIGHT.

[SFX: clockwork; a storm building outside]

NARRATOR: The wind has been rising since noon. Mirela winds the clockwork early and watches the harbour lights go out one by one below her.

MIRELA: Come on, then. Say something.

[SFX: static, then the Listener]

THE LISTENER: Open the book, Mirela. Before the bell.

NARRATOR: Mirela's brown eyes go to the logbook on its shelf, and to the brass key lying beside it.

SCENE 2 - THE RADIO SHED. NIGHT.

[SFX: valves; rain on a tin roof]

NARRATOR: Ilsabeth has the receiver in pieces on the bench, a valve in each hand.

ILSABET: Keats is gone, Shelley's going, and the Listener is louder than I have ever heard it.

MIRELA: What is it saying?

ILSABET: Names. Boats. It keeps saying the Gannet.

SCENE 3 - THE QUAY. NIGHT.

[SFX: the curfew bell, three strokes; waves against stone]

NARRATOR: Teodor has not come home. Odile Marr's boat is still out past the point, her engine dead, her lamp a smear in the rain.

SORREL: Nobody goes. You know the rule.

MIRELA: My brother is on that boat.

NARRATOR: They pushed the boat out after the curfew bell, oars muffled with rags, Sorrel shouting from the quay behind them.

SCENE 4 - THE GANNET, PAST THE POINT. NIGHT.

[SFX: storm; the Gannet's hull groaning]

ODILE: The line's fouled the screw. If someone doesn't clear it we'll be on the rocks in ten minutes.

NARRATOR: Teodor dove into the deep water without a second thought, the knife between his teeth, and cleared the line in one breath.

BRANNOCH: Good lad. Now get back aboard before she turns.

SCENE 5 - THE LAMP ROOM. DAWN.

[SFX: the storm easing; clockwork running down]

NARRATOR: The Gannet comes home with the dawn. Up in the lamp room, the key is gone from beside the logbook, and the clasp hangs open.
