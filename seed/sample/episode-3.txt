SCENE 1 - THE RADIO SHED. NIGHT.

[SFX: valves; a pencil scratching]

ILSABET: Shelley's flickering. Byron's fine. Hold still, all of you, I'm recording.

MIRELA: You believe me, then.

ILSABET: I believe something is transmitting on one point seven. I don't believe in ghosts. Those are different sentences.

[SFX: static; the Listener's voice, faint]

THE LISTENER: Ilsabet Rhee. You still owe Sorrel an apology for the Midsummer fuse.

ILSABET: That's not possible. Nobody knows about the fuse.

MIRELA: Welcome to my week.

SCENE 2 - THE GANNET, AT SEA. DAY.

[SFX: engine; spray; gulls]

ODILE: Line, Teodor. Coil it, don't strangle it.

TEODOR: It's coiled. It's coiled with respect.

ODILE: Your sister was up the light all night again.

TEODOR: She hears a voice on the radio. She thinks it's someone who knew Brannoch.

ODILE: And what do you think?

TEODOR: I think I don't like anything that comes out of the sea talking.

[SFX: a wave slapping the hull; Teodor gasps and grabs the rail]

ODILE: Easy. You're on the boat.

TEODOR: The boat is where the water isn't. The boat is where the water isn't.

SCENE 3 - HOLLOW QUAY. DUSK.

[SFX: broken timber groaning; the curfew bell, far off]

WREN: Keeper! I found something. It costs five pieces.

MIRELA: You are not supposed to be out here, Wren. Not on the Quay.

WREN: It was under the salt line. On the old step. Four pieces, final offer.

[SFX: a small metal object pressed into a palm]

NARRATOR: It is a brass key, green at the edges, the right size for a clasp.

MIRELA: Where did you get this?

WREN: I told you. Under the salt. Three pieces, because you look sad.

SCENE 4 - THE LAMP ROOM. NIGHT.

[SFX: clockwork; rain easing]

NARRATOR: Mirela sets the key beside the logbook and does not use it. The lamp turns. The harbour sleeps. On the old channel, for the first time in a week, there is nothing but static.

MIRELA: Not tonight. When I'm ready. Not tonight.
