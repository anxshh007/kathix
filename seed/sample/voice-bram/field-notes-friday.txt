Friday. Clear sky! First one all week. I nearly didn't recognise it.

The kid with the sea glass found me again. She charges for everything — directions, gossip, the time. Two green pieces for the time. I paid. What else was I going to do?

Asked her about the radio shed. She said don't. I said why? She said because the radio talks back. I laughed. She didn't. Kids here don't laugh at the right things. Or maybe I don't.

Evening: sat on the wall by the inn and watched the light come on. You hear it before you see it — a click, a hum, then the beam swings out over the water like it's checking on everybody. I don't believe in much. I might believe in that.
