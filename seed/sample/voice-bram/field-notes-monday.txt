Monday. Up at five. Cold. The kettle's dead again — third time this month. I'm not fixing it. I'm not.

Walked the north path to the quay. Wind straight off the sea, the kind that gets inside your coat and stays there. Saw two seals. Maybe three? One kept ducking under before I could count it. Rude.

The ferry's late. It's always late. You'd think after twenty years they'd move the timetable. They won't. Nobody here moves anything. That's the whole charm, apparently.

Got talking to the harbourmaster. Big hands, small patience. She asked what I'm writing. I said notes. She said notes about what? I said you. She didn't laugh. I did.

Lunch: bread, cheese, an apple that's seen things. Notes: see above. Plan: sleep.
