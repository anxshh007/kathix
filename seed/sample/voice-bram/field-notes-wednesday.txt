Wednesday. Rain. Obviously.

Went up the lighthouse today — finally! The keeper let me in if I promised not to touch anything. I touched one thing. A rail. Who doesn't touch a rail?

The stair's ridiculous. I lost count somewhere past eighty and she just kept going, counting out loud, like the steps might complain if she didn't. Strange habit. I liked it.

Top of the tower: glass everywhere, the lamp ticking like a big brass clock. You can see the whole bay. You can see the weather coming. You can see your own life looking small and wet and very far down.

She asked if I was scared of heights. I said no. Liar. She knew. She didn't say. That's twice now someone here has been kind to me without making a thing of it. I'm keeping count.
