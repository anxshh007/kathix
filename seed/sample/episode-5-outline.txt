- Dawn after the storm. Mirela finds the logbook open in the lamp room and the brass key missing.
- She reads the last page: Brannoch wrote that he cut the Gannet loose because he heard a voice on the old channel telling him to.
- Ilsabet traces the one point seven signal to Hollow Quay, where nothing should be transmitting.
- Wren admits she sold the key to someone in a Tidecaller's coat for a whole jar of sea glass.
- Mirela keeps the lamp burning through the next night while Ilsabet listens, and the Listener speaks to both of them at once.
- The Listener says one new thing: the voice Brannoch heard was not the same voice they hear now.
