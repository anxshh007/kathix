SCENE 1 - GANNET'S REST. NIGHT.

[SFX: a fire that never goes out; low voices; a glass set down]

NARRATOR: Three years ago, on the night they now call the Long Night, Brannoch Hale sat at this table and finished his tea before the storm came for him.

ODILE: He told me to stay in harbour. Said the Gannet would ride it out on her lines.

MIRELA: And then he went out and cut her loose.

ODILE: I never understood it. She was found two days later, drifting, not a scratch on her. And him gone.

SCENE 2 - FLASHBACK. THE QUAY, THE LONG NIGHT.

[SFX: a storm at full strength; rope straining; timber cracking]

BRANNOCH: Mira! Get back up the stair and keep that lamp lit, whatever you see!

MIRELA: You can't row in this!

BRANNOCH: I'm not rowing far, girl. The lamp. Never let it go dark.

[SFX: a wave breaking over stone; the jetty of Hollow Quay giving way]

NARRATOR: That was the last thing Old Brannoch said to anyone. Hollow Quay broke under him and the sea took the rest.

SCENE 3 - THE LAMP ROOM. PRESENT. NIGHT.

[SFX: clockwork; rain]

NARRATOR: Mirela keeps his logbook on the shelf beside the lamp: oilcloth, a brass clasp, locked. She has never opened it. Some nights she rests her hand on it while the clockwork runs down.

[SFX: static rising]

THE LISTENER: He wrote about you, you know. The last page.

MIRELA: You don't get to talk about him.

THE LISTENER: The key is under the salt.

[SFX: the static cuts out]

SCENE 4 - THE KEEPER'S STEP. DAWN.

[SFX: footsteps; a door; the sea]

NARRATOR: In the morning there is a line of salt across the keeper's step. The Tidecallers leave it every year on the anniversary of the drowning. Mirela had forgotten the date. The sea had not.

SORREL: Tidecallers' salt. Leave it be, Voss. It's for him.

MIRELA: I know who it's for, Sorrel.

SORREL: Then you'll also know what he'd say about you sitting up all night listening to static instead of watching your light.

MIRELA: The light didn't go dark.

SORREL: Not yet.

NARRATOR: When the harbourmaster has gone, Mirela kneels by the step. She does not touch the salt. But she looks at it for a long time, and thinks about keys.
