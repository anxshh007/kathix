# The Lantern Keepers — Story Bible

An original audio drama written for the Kathix sample project. A lighthouse keeper on a storm-cut coast hears a voice on the harbour radio that nobody else can hear.

## Character: Mirela Voss
Aliases: Mira
Keeper of the Saltmarsh Light since her mother retired. Steady, dry-humoured, keeps everyone's secrets but her own.
- Eyes: grey
- Role: lighthouse keeper
- Age: 34
Related: Teodor Voss (sister of); Ilsabet Rhee (friend of); Brannoch Hale (apprentice of)

## Character: Teodor Voss
Aliases: Teo
Mirela's younger brother, apprentice to the ferry. Loud, kind, and terrified of the sea he works on.
- Fears: deep water
- Cannot: swim
- Role: ferry apprentice
Related: Mirela Voss (brother of); Odile Marr (apprentice to)

## Character: Ilsabet Rhee
Runs the harbour radio from a shed full of valves and tea tins. First to admit she cannot explain the Listener.
- Role: radio operator
- Habit: names every valve after a dead poet
Related: Mirela Voss (friend of)

## Character: Brannoch Hale
Aliases: Old Brannoch
The keeper before Mirela's mother. Taught Mirela the lamp. Went out in the storm of the Long Night to cut the Gannet loose and did not come back.
- Status: drowned in the Long Night storm (Episode 2)
- Left behind: a locked logbook
Related: Mirela Voss (teacher of)

## Character: Odile Marr
Aliases: Captain Marr
Skipper of the supply boat the Gannet. Has run the crossing for twenty years and never once been late.
- Role: skipper of the Gannet
- Voice: low, unhurried

## Character: Sorrel Pike
Harbourmaster. Rings the curfew bell at dusk and believes rules are what keep people alive.
- Role: harbourmaster
Related: Ilsabet Rhee (rival of)

## Character: Wren Calloway
Nine years old. Collects sea glass and trades it for favours, food and secrets.
- Age: 9
Related: Mirela Voss (pesters)

## Character: The Listener
A voice on the harbour radio, heard only at night, that knows things it should not.
- First heard: Episode 1
- Heard by: Mirela, and later Ilsabet

## Place: Saltmarsh Light
The lighthouse on the spit at the mouth of Kessock Harbour. The lamp room is reached by a spiral stair.
- Steps: 112
- Lamp: clockwork, wound every four hours

## Place: Kessock Harbour
The village and harbour below the light. Forty houses, one inn, one radio shed.

## Place: Gannet's Rest
The harbour inn. Its fire never goes out, and its landlord never asks questions.

## Place: Hollow Quay
The ruined jetty north of the harbour, broken by the Long Night storm. Children are told to keep away.

## Object: The Gannet
Odile Marr's supply boat. Brannoch cut it loose in the storm and it was found drifting, unharmed, two days later.

## Object: Brannoch's logbook
A keeper's logbook bound in oilcloth and locked with a brass clasp. Mirela keeps it in the lamp room and has never opened it.

## Object: The radio set
Ilsabet's receiver, built from salvage. Picks up the Listener on a frequency nobody uses.

## Faction: The Harbour Watch
Volunteers who keep the boats safe at night. Led by Sorrel Pike.

## Faction: The Tidecallers
The old fishing families, who still leave salt on the step for the drowned.

## Rule: The curfew bell
When the harbourmaster rings the curfew bell at dusk, the harbour is closed until dawn.
- Forbidden: boat, after the curfew bell

## Rule: The lamp never goes dark
The Saltmarsh Light must burn from dusk to dawn, every night, whatever happens.
- Forbidden: lamp, dark

## Rule: Salt for the drowned
On the anniversary of a drowning, the Tidecallers leave a line of salt on the keeper's step.

## Fact: The Long Night
The storm that broke Hollow Quay and took Brannoch. Nobody in Kessock calls it by any other name.

## Fact: The Listener's frequency
The Listener speaks on 1.7 megahertz, a channel the harbour stopped using years ago.

## Event: The Long Night storm
The storm strikes Kessock Harbour and breaks Hollow Quay.
When: Late autumn, three years before the story | 1

## Event: Brannoch drowns
Brannoch rows out to cut the Gannet loose and is lost.
When: Second night of the Long Night storm | 2

## Event: The Listener first speaks
Mirela hears the Listener say her mother's name.
When: Episode 1, three years after the storm | 3

## Gag: Wren's sea glass economy
Wren pays for everything in sea glass and insists on the exchange rate.

## Promise: Who is the Listener?
The voice on the radio knows things only the drowned could know. The audience is owed an answer.
Planted: Episode 1

## Promise: The locked logbook
Brannoch's logbook stays locked. Mirela will open it when she is ready, and it will matter.
Planted: Episode 2
