The harbour was quiet when she came down from the light, quieter than it had been in weeks, and the boats lay still against their moorings as though they had been told some secret and were keeping it; she walked the length of the quay slowly, listening to the water move beneath the boards, and thought about the voice on the radio, and the key, and the long patience of the sea, which forgets nothing and forgives very little, and which had, she suspected, been waiting for her all along.
